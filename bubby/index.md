# Bubby

Bubby helps parents log sleep, feeds, diapers, pumping and growth, see when the next nap is likely to be, and share one log with a partner or caregiver. It can also import a child's growth measurements from their health record through MyChart, and create a printable summary for a pediatrician visit.

Your information stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. See the [Privacy Policy](/bubby/privacy/) for details, and the [Terms of Use](/bubby/terms/).

## Common questions

**How do I share with my partner?**
Go to Settings, then Invite a Caregiver, and send the invitation with Apple's sharing sheet. Your partner opens the link on their iPhone while signed in to iCloud.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Go to Settings, then Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on the phone. For data shared through iCloud, remove it under Settings, your Apple Account, iCloud, Manage Storage.

**How do I get a report for the pediatrician?**
Go to Settings, then Export and Share with Pediatrician. You can share a PDF summary or a CSV of everything you have logged.

**Why can't I start a nap while nursing?**
Baby can only do one thing at a time: sleep, nursing, a bottle, solids or a diaper change. Stop the one that is running first. Pumping is the exception. You can pump during any of them.

## Important

Bubby is not medical advice. Talk to your pediatrician about any concerns about your child's health.
