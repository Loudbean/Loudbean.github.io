# Bubby Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Bubby ("the app") is published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we"). This page explains what information the app handles and where it goes. Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

## The short version

- We do not run any servers for this app, and it has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.
- What you log stays on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you choose to share with caregivers.
- We cannot see your baby's information.

## What the app stores

When you use the app you may enter: your baby's name, birth date and (optionally) sex and photo; feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping and growth measurements; and notes. If you choose to enter a name for yourself in Settings, it is saved with the entries you log so other caregivers can see who logged them. This is stored in the app's database on your iPhone.

## Sharing with caregivers (iCloud)

If you invite a caregiver, the baby's log is stored in your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit) and shared with the people you invite, using Apple's sharing sheet. That data is held by Apple under your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's terms. We have no access to it. You can stop sharing, or remove a caregiver, at any time from the sharing screen. Caregivers you invite can see and add to the log.

## Importing growth from a health record (optional)

If you choose "Import Growth from MyChart", you sign in on your healthcare provider's own MyChart page (the app opens it in a secure browser window). The app receives a temporary access permission and reads your child's weight, length and head-circumference measurements once. They are saved on your iPhone. To let you choose your clinic, the app downloads Epic's public list of clinics (open.epic.com); nothing about you or your child is sent in that request. The app does not store your MyChart sign-in or the access permission, and nothing from the import is sent to us. Your provider's own privacy policy applies to your health record.

## Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Widgets and Live Activities read the same on-device data as the app. We do not send you push notifications from a server. If you use caregiver sharing, Apple may send silent background updates so the log stays in step.

## Exporting

The app can create a PDF summary or a CSV file of your log. These files are created on your iPhone and go only where you choose to send them (for example email, Files, or a message to your pediatrician).

## Purchases

A one-time purchase unlocks premium features. It is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

## Information we collect

None. We do not collect, sell or share personal information, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising.

## Your choices

- Edit or delete any entry in the app.
- Delete all data by deleting the app. Data stored in iCloud for sharing can be removed in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account, iCloud, Manage Storage.
- Stop sharing, or leave a log shared with you, in the app's Settings.

## Children

The app is for parents and caregivers. It stores information about a baby that the parent enters. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Not medical advice

Nap windows, growth percentiles and reminders are general guidance and not medical advice. Talk to your pediatrician about any concerns.

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
